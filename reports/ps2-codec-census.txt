Codec block census, PlayStation 2 (2004), SLPS-25400
====================================================

Scope, stated rather than implied.

  Scanned      TOSROOT, TOSBTL, TOSCHT, TOSFIELD
               1,007 files, 498,571,983 bytes -- the game's structured data
  Not scanned  TOSMOV, TOSEV, TOSSND, TOSCV
               206 files, 2,700,466,560 bytes -- movies, event scenes,
               voice and music, all streamed media

  TOSROOT, TOSBTL and TOSCHT were scanned at every 32-byte boundary with the
  default per-file decode budget of 64 MB.  TOSFIELD would not finish under
  those settings -- its data produces enough false headers that a
  quarter-megabyte file can take a minute -- so it was SAMPLED at every
  256-byte boundary with a 2 MB budget.  That is enough to rule out a large
  population of blocks in it and not enough to rule out a few.

  Both GameCube discs, by contrast, are covered in full at 32-byte steps and
  never reach the budget: see gc-codec-census.txt and gc-codec-census-d2.txt.

  Files the budget abandoned are named in each section below.  D.D and
  NPC_ALL.BIN were rescanned afterwards without a meaningful bound; see
  ps2-btlenemy-probe.txt.
